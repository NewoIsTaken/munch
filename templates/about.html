About MUNCH

Have you ever asked yourself, "Hmmm, I wonder what's for dinner today?" or "Do I eat the food in the dining hall, or do I eat out?" Well, look no further, because Munch is here to help. Take the stress of guessing what is going to be yummy off of your plate. With Munch, you can easily assess where the best food across campus is — and what that food will be!
